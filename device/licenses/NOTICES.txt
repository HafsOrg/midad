Data (built into midad/data/ and midad/qml/)

                           KFGQPC Hafs v22 text from nuqayah/qpc-fonts (same commit as above);
                           King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex terms apply.
  Quran search index and surah names (index.json, surahs.json)
                           Derived from the quran-png bundle (index/words.json, index/surahs.json),
                           https://github.com/quran-ws/quran-png release artwork-v1.0.0,
                           file quran-svg-hafs-kfgqpc.tar.gz,
                           sha256 2bff7bcc1d84b61ee9baf2c356ea554b0a1dd317d6a8145406b5cee998914d17.
                           Its VERSION.json (quran-png-VERSION.json) says: CC BY 4.0 covers the
                           decomposition, labels and indexes; the Quranic text and artwork are the
                           KFGQPC's and not licensed by it. Its LICENSE (quran-png-LICENSE.txt)
                           is a placeholder that states no terms.
  Hadith text (hadith.json, hadith-index.json)
                           Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim matns and chapter titles from
                           ShathaTm/LK-Hadith-Corpus at c45fa64aa40bf1d4a34b9b35359640a583b02881
                           (no licence stated upstream); Muslim numbering from
                           fawazahmed0/hadith-api at df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784,
                           editions/ara-muslim.min.json (Unlicense, see hadith-api-LICENSE.txt).

Because several of these carry no licence or restrictive terms, the Nix flake fetches
the upstream sources and builds the data on the installing machine; check the terms
above before redistributing a built bundle.
